# Physics

GCSE → research level. The best training there is in modelling the real world with mathematics. In the current
trajectory physics is the **disciplined secondary**: climbed steadily (a block every other Sunday, plus a weekly Isaac
Physics warm-up), for its own sake and for what it does to your mathematical maturity. If it becomes the thing that
lights you up, swap it into the spine at a quarterly gate.

**How to use this file.** Strictly top to bottom; `today` shows the first unticked session. From P05 on, physics leans
hard on the maths track — do the paired maths first. **Levels:** F · S · U · G · R (see `CURRICULUM.md`).

## P01 · Placement & GCSE Mechanics and Energy
<!-- rung: F | prereq: none | feeds: everything in this track -->
**Goal:** Find your level, then make the basics of motion, force and energy solid and intuitive.
**Resources:**
- [Isaac Physics](https://isaacscience.org/) — free problems, GCSE to university (from Cambridge)
- [Physics & Maths Tutor — GCSE Physics](https://www.physicsandmathstutor.com/physics-revision/gcse-aqa/)
- [BBC Bitesize](https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize) — GCSE Physics
- [Khan Academy Physics](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/physics)
**Sessions:**
- [ ] P01.S01 — Placement: a GCSE Physics paper from [PMT](https://www.physicsandmathstutor.com/physics-revision/gcse-aqa/), timed and marked. List the gaps; tick what you own in P01–P02 ("placed out").
- [ ] P01.S02 — Motion: distance and displacement, speed and velocity, acceleration, motion graphs — derive the SUVAT equations.
- [ ] P01.S03 — Forces: resultant forces, Newton's three laws, weight vs mass, terminal velocity.
- [ ] P01.S04 — Momentum and its conservation; stopping distances.
- [ ] P01.S05 — Energy: stores and transfers, work, power, efficiency; kinetic and gravitational potential energy.
- [ ] P01.S06 — Home experiment: measure g with a pendulum and your phone's stopwatch; write it up with uncertainties.
**Gate artifact:** SUVAT derived from definitions + the pendulum experiment write-up with uncertainties.
**Anki seeds:** SUVAT equations · Newton's laws · energy formulas · momentum conservation
