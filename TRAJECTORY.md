# TRAJECTORY — who this turns you into, and by when

## The honest reframe

"PhD-level in maths, physics, CS, finance, and AI, self-taught, in ten years" — taken literally, no. A PhD is ~4
focused years *each*; five of them in parallel isn't a plan, it's a wish, and chasing it produces five shallow
fractions of one.

But the instinct underneath it is right, and there's a version that is both **more valuable** and **actually
reachable**:

> **Research-level depth in one chosen intersection, strong undergraduate-to-graduate command of the fields that feed
> it, and a decade-deep public portfolio that proves all of it — a body of work most PhDs don't have.**

That is worth more than a certificate, and it's the thing that opens the doors you're aiming at: principal engineer,
quant researcher, low-latency specialist, founder, or — if you still want it then — a PhD you walk into with a
portfolio instead of just a transcript.

## The spine (recommended — confirm or change it)

Everything you listed already points one way. Your goal has been **senior fintech / low-latency / SRE**; you trade;
you're building systems. So the spine that makes the whole thing cohere:

**Computer Science & Systems · Mathematics · AI/ML · Quantitative Finance** — four tracks that reinforce each other,
converging on the intersection of **low-latency systems × quantitative/ML-driven markets**. That is a rare, highly
paid, intellectually deep niche, and it uses almost everything you listed.

**Physics** becomes the *disciplined secondary*: the best possible training in modelling and mathematical maturity,
climbed steadily for its own sake and its transfer — not raced to research level unless you later decide it *is* the
spine. **Economics** rides along inside the finance track.

> This is the one real decision. If physics (or AI research, or pure maths) is what actually lights you up, we swap
> the spine and re-sequence — the system doesn't change, only the emphasis does.

## The milestones

Assuming the daily system holds (~90 min/day floor, more when you have it). Each milestone is a *capability + an
artifact*, never "I covered the material."

### Year 1 — foundations down, first real credential
- **Spine:** maths through early-undergrad (solid calculus, linear algebra done properly, intro proof & probability);
  CS core (DSA with benchmarks, OS & networking basics); Rust to competence; ML classical + *build a small GPT*;
  finance foundations + **one honest backtest** of your own strategy.
- **Flagship:** DataPulse through its SRE phases — enough to **land a senior-track SRE/platform role in fintech.**
- **Proof:** a repo with a year of commits, a working observable system, and write-ups. The habit is now who you are.

### Year 3 — undergraduate depth, a visible specialist
- Real analysis & measure-theoretic probability; distributed systems (Raft implemented); deep learning from scratch;
  derivatives pricing (Black–Scholes three ways). Stochastic calculus begun — the bridge to quant.
- **Flagship:** the **low-latency track** — a Rust ingest/matching path measured with real tail-latency distributions.
- **Proof:** contributing to open source; a portfolio that reads as "early-career quant/low-latency engineer," not
  "learner." Likely a role at that intersection.

### Year 5 — graduate-level in the spine, recognised in a niche
- Graduate command of 1–2 spine areas (stochastic calculus + its ML/quant uses; distributed & low-latency systems).
- **Flagship:** a genuinely impressive crossover system — e.g. a low-latency, observable, ML-or-signal-driven trading
  stack — *with* research-grade write-ups (benchmarks, postmortems, a methods note).
- **Proof:** the repo is a five-year public body of work that functions as a credential on its own. Optionality opens:
  senior quant/engineer, principal, PhD-with-a-portfolio, or your own thing.

### Year 10 — research-level at the intersection
- A real contribution in your chosen niche (the ML × quant × low-latency-systems crossroad), read and written at paper
  level. Broad graduate literacy across the rest; physics climbed to a satisfying depth.
- **Proof:** a decade-deep public portfolio, a reputation in the niche, and the free choice of what comes next —
  earned, not credentialed.

## How the milestones stay honest

Milestones are checked at the **quarterly gate** (see [SYSTEM](SYSTEM.md)). Every quarter: did I clear a gate and
produce its artifact? The trajectory is re-read and *edited* each quarter — if life compresses the time, the
milestones slide; they don't get faked. A roadmap you revise is alive; one you don't is decoration.

> The decade is not the point. The daily block is the point. Do that, and the decade takes care of itself.
